Duet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Duet helps you practice reading aloud. Your custom passages and settings are stored on your device. Reader profiles and practice progress may also sync through your Apple iCloud account. Duet has no developer-operated server that receives your profiles, passages, or practice recordings.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, Duet listens while you read so it can check your reading. Your speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, which may happen on your device or on Apple's servers depending on your device, under Apple's privacy policy. Duet does not record, save, or send your audio anywhere else.

Custom passages and settings

Passages you add, your app settings, and the reader selected on this device are stored locally. Duet does not publish your passages or share them with other users. You can delete a custom passage by swiping its row on the home screen and choosing Delete.

Reader profiles, progress, and iCloud

Duet stores each reader's name, animal avatar, scores, practice times, attempts, and lesson selections. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit may synchronize these profiles and progress across devices using the same Apple ID. Custom passage text is not included in Duet's CloudKit synchronization. Apple processes iCloud data under Apple's privacy policy. Without iCloud, Duet keeps profiles and progress on the device.

Deleting your data

To delete a reader and their progress, open the reader picker, long-press the reader, choose Delete, and confirm Delete reader and their progress. The deletion also synchronizes to your other devices when iCloud synchronization is available. Deleting the app removes its local app data, but does not by itself remove data already stored in iCloud. Delete the reader in Duet before uninstalling if you also want to remove their synchronized profile and progress.

No tracking, ads, or accounts

Duet has no app registration or login, ads, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking. Reader profiles do not require passwords or email addresses. Optional iCloud synchronization uses the Apple ID configured in your device's system settings.

Children

The data handling described above also applies to children using Duet. A parent or guardian can manage and delete a child's reader profile and progress. Duet does not use this information for advertising or tracking.
