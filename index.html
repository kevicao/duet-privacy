Duet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Duet helps you practice reading aloud. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, Duet listens while you read so it can check your reading. Your speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, which may happen on your device or on Apple's servers depending on your device, under Apple's privacy policy. Duet does not record, save, or send your audio anywhere else.

Data on your device

Your settings, any passages you add, and your best scores are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

No tracking, ads, or accounts

Duet has no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Children

Duet does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
